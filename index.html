Before you ask.

Is it legal where I live?

Limits differ by window and vehicle type. We check the current rules during the consult and will not cut a film that fails them.

How long until I can use the windows?

You can drive home the same day. Leave the windows up for three days so the film cures properly.

What does the warranty cover?

Fading, bubbling, peeling and discolouring for as long as you own the car. Damage from cleaning with abrasives is excluded.

Will it affect my phone signal?

Not with ceramic film. It contains no metal, so radio, GPS and toll tags work as normal.
